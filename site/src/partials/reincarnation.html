04Reincarnation at ~200k

The context window may be larger, but JIVA caps each session at 200k tokens on purpose. Past that, quality drops before the window runs out.

  1. 150k (75%): the watchdog types a nudge into the pane: write your handoff.
  2. The session runs jiva handoff: ≤40 lines of pointers, not a transcript. Then it stops.
  3. The watchdog sees it idle and respawns the pane. The SessionStart hook hands the new body its handoff and task card.
  4. 200k without a handoff trips the ceiling breaker: one hard message, then a flag for you.

The kill switch

$ touch .jiva/STOP

The watchdog checks for this file every tick and exits when it appears. No respawns, no nudges. The tmux bar reads STOPPED. just down kills the private tmux server and the dashboard; your own tmux is untouched.

Breakers

Seven trip wires write a file to .jiva/flags/: repeat (the same tool call 4 times in a row), storm (past 150k within 3 minutes of boot, so the session is parked), ceiling, burn (60k tokens without a comb line), time (a life past 90 minutes), usd (a life past $15) and heal-storm (3 respawns inside 5 minutes: the 4th is refused and the session parked). The burn, time and usd limits are env-tunable. A parked session stays down until you remove .jiva/parked/<name>. jiva doctor breakers trips each one on purpose.

Some events also ping the desk: a parked session, a pane stuck on a prompt, a full disk or a near-empty quota. The watchdog sources core/notify.sh, so every toast lands in .jiva/notify.log, then tries a WSL balloon, notify-send, osascript or a terminal bell. Nothing phones home.
