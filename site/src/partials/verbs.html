03Six verbs

Sessions coordinate through six verbs on the jiva command (just j-* wraps them) and one append-only comb. That's the whole protocol. Anything a verb can't express goes to a keeper. The desk has two more: jiva answer closes an ask, jiva pending lists panes stuck at a prompt.

  1. claim <paths>Lock directories before editing. Overlapping claims are refused.
  2. report done|blocked|notedone runs jiva check first: files ≤150 lines, notes ≤30, scripts parse.
  3. ask <q> <opts> <default>A typed question for the desk, with a class. Always carries a default.
  4. ship <msg>Commits only the claimed paths, pushes under a lock.
  5. handoff≤40 lines of pointers to the next body, then stop.
  6. inboxThe last 15 comb lines plus open desk asks.
