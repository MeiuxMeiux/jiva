06The watchdog view

just dash serves a one-page dashboard on 127.0.0.1, on a random port between 20000 and 60000. It reads state.json every 5 seconds, greys out if the data is older than 30 seconds, and goes red when anything is flagged. An Alerts panel lists the newest notify.log lines, and an alert under 10 minutes old raises an amber banner. Prefer the terminal? jiva watch redraws the status line, sessions, flags, open asks, an ALERTS line and the last board lines every 2 seconds; press a to answer an ask.
