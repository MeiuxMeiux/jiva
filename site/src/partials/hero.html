public alpha · runs on your machine

One terminal.
Many souls.

JIVA keeps a small hive of Claude Code sessions alive in a private tmux server. A desk talks to you, a queen splits the work, workers do it. A plain bash watchdog checks every session every 5 seconds and swaps any of them for a fresh one before it gets past 200k tokens of context.

You keep one window open. Questions reach you there. The rest of the hive stays out of your way.
